Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think and learn.
Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI that enables systems to automatically improve from experience without explicit programming.
Deep Learning is a subset of machine learning that uses neural networks with many layers.
Natural Language Processing (NLP) allows computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language.
Reinforcement Learning is an area of ML where an agent learns to make decisions by taking actions in an environment to maximize some reward.
Computer Vision enables machines to interpret and make decisions based on visual data.
