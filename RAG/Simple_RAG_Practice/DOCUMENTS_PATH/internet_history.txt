The Internet originated from the ARPANET project funded by the U.S. Department of Defense in the late 1960s.
The first message sent over ARPANET was "LO" (an attempt to log in to a remote computer from UCLA to Stanford).
Tim Berners-Lee invented the World Wide Web in 1989 while working at CERN.
The first website was created in 1991 and explained what the World Wide Web was.
Email existed before the World Wide Web; the first email was sent in 1971 by Ray Tomlinson.
The first web browser, called WorldWideWeb, was also created by Tim Berners-Lee.
