Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns, mainly caused by human activities, especially burning fossil fuels.
The Earth's average temperature has risen about 1.2°C since the late 19th century.
Carbon dioxide (CO2) levels are at their highest in at least 800,000 years.
The last decade (2010-2019) was the warmest on record.
Melting ice sheets in Greenland and Antarctica are contributing to sea level rise.
Renewable energy sources like solar and wind are key to mitigating climate change.
