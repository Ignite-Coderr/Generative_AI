## Security Considerations in Multimodal LLMs

The deployment of multimodal large language models (LLMs) introduces unique privacy and security challenges that must be addressed to ensure data integrity and user protection. Multimodal systems, which process and interpret both textual and visual data, can inadvertently expose sensitive information. This exposure often occurs through user interactions in various environments, where subtle nuances in interpretation may lead to unintended data disclosure.
